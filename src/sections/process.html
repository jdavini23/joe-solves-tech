How it works

Email, a diagnostic, then the credit.

Site Rescue stays in your inbox. Three days from the day you write.

01

Email the website

It takes under five minutes. Business name, website, what you sell, the one thing bugging you, what you want to happen (more calls booked, less busywork), what you have tried, the tools you use, and any budget or deadline.

02

The diagnostic comes back

One page, in plain English, within three days. What I looked at: the site, forms, tools, and follow-up. Three to five findings, ranked by what each costs in money or time. The one fix I would do first, and why. Three fixed-price options, A, B, and C. Each has a price, what is included, a timeline, and what is left out. The prices are quoted in the diagnostic, never by the hour. A short screen recording if it helps.

03

The fee comes off the fix

Reply with A, B, or C. I send a one-page agreement and an invoice for 50% up front. Work starts when the deposit lands. The balance is due on delivery. The $149 comes off the fix.

You own every account and login from day one.
