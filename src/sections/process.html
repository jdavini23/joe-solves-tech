How it works

Three steps. No deck, no discovery phase.

You are busy running the thing. The whole process is built so you spend about an hour on it in total.

STEP 01

Tell me the task

Twenty minutes on a call. You describe the part of the week you dread. I ask what happens before it and after it.

STEP 02

I build it

About a week. You get one message partway through with something you can click, so nothing is a surprise at the end.

STEP 03

You own it

Every account is in your name from day one. You get a short walkthrough video so whoever comes after me can pick it up.
