Questions

The things people ask me first.

What's in the diagnostic?

One page, in plain English. It covers what I looked at, three to five findings ranked by what they cost you, and the one fix I would do first. You get three options, A, B, and C. Each one has a fixed price, what's included, and a timeline. It also says what's not included, and what happens if you do nothing. Sometimes a short screen recording walks through it.

What if I don't want the fix?

You keep the diagnostic. The $149 only comes off the price if you go ahead with A, B, or C.

Why email and no calls?

Everything is written down. You can read it when it suits you, and nothing gets lost in a call.

When do I pay?

The $149 is paid up front, before I start the diagnostic. For the fix, half is due up front when you sign the one-page agreement. Work starts when the deposit lands. The balance is due on delivery. Fixed price, never hourly.

What happens if you disappear?

Every account is created in your name and your email, on day one, not handed over at the end. I don't hold a login you can't reset without me. You get a walkthrough video so the next person can pick the site up cold. I lost a business to a developer who did the opposite, so this one is not negotiable for me.
