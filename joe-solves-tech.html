Questions

The things people ask me first.

I don't know what to automate. Where do we even start?

That is the normal starting point, and it's what the call is for. You don't need to arrive with a spec. Tell me which hour of your week you dread most and we work backwards from there. Nine times out of ten the answer is retyping something that already exists somewhere else.

What happens if you disappear?

Every account is created in your name and your email, on day one, not handed over at the end. I don't hold a single login you can't reset without me. You also get a walkthrough video so the next person can pick it up cold. I lost a business to a developer who did the opposite, so this one is not negotiable for me.

Do I have to switch off the tools I already use?

No. I would rather connect what you have than sell you a migration. If you run on spreadsheets and a shared inbox, we build around spreadsheets and a shared inbox. The only time I suggest moving is when the tool itself is the thing costing you the hours, and then I will say so plainly and let you decide.

What does it cost, and when do I pay?

Fixed price, agreed on the call before anything starts. Half up front, half when it works and you have signed off. If it doesn't do what we wrote down, you don't pay the second half and you keep what I built.
